## Best for Newborns and Infants

### 1. Prosper Pediatrics

Dr. Scott Chowning founded Prosper Pediatrics back in 2014, and it's become a go-to for families with newborns. He's board-certified with the American Academy of Pediatrics and keeps his patient panel tight, which means you're not just a number. The practice sees kids from newborn to age 21, and Chowning's known for having an excellent bedside manner with anxious parents and fussy babies alike. Parents consistently note short wait times (we're talking under 15 minutes most days) and that he actually listens to your concerns rather than rushing through visits.

The office is at 1000 N. Preston Rd., Suite 20, and they're open Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., plus Saturday mornings. They even offer house calls in Prosper and Celina, which is a game-changer if you're exhausted and don't want to haul a newborn into the clinic. If you need pediatric care, you'll find [more healthcare professionals in Prosper here](/professionals/).

**New Patients:** Accepting

**Insurance:** Check with your provider, but they work with most major plans

**Standout Feature:** Weekend hours and in-home newborn visits

### 2. Centennial Pediatrics (Prosper Location)

Centennial's been around for over 20 years serving North Texas, and their Prosper office on Legacy Drive handles newborn care with real expertise. They offer well visits, vaccines, and the full spectrum of preventive care that new families need. The practice is spacious and welcoming, and they've got multiple providers so you can usually get in quickly even when it's busy. Many families appreciate that they accept a wide range of insurance plans and are upfront about billing.

Located at 940 South Legacy, Suite 20, Centennial is another solid choice for first-time parents. They've built their reputation on being thorough during those newborn visits and answering the hundred questions you'll have in those first weeks.

**New Patients:** Accepting (verify at 214-389-8801)

If you've got kids heading to kindergarten or already in elementary school, Cook Children's Prosper Trail location is worth considering. They handle well-child visits, school physicals, sports physicals, and immunizations. The practice stays organized and efficient, which matters when you're juggling schedules with multiple kids. The team knows how to handle that nervous first-grader coming in for shots, and they've got experience with everything from ear infections to treating injuries.

They're located at 1350 N. Preston Road, Suite 40, and they offer same-day appointments for pressing needs, which is huge when your kid wakes up with an ear infection the day before their soccer tournament. New families moving to Prosper often overlook this practice, but it's been a steady, reliable choice for school-age pediatric care.

**New Patients:** Accepting (call 817-347-4100)

This is the newer player in town (opened in 2023), and it's built on a large campus just north of Dallas that sits on nearly 75 acres. For school-age kids, this means if you need to see a specialist, you're not playing phone tag with different offices.

The primary care team at Centennial is solid, and they're set up to handle the growth in the area. Parents appreciate the modern facility and the fact that everything feels new and well-organized.
