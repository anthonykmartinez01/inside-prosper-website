## Best for Color & Highlights

<img src="/images/blog/salons-color.webp" alt="Woman getting hair color treatment at Prosper TX salon" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin:1.5rem 0;" />

### 1. Gloss Salon & Co

Located right on the south side of Preston Road at 120 S Preston Rd #30, Gloss Salon & Co is the spot if you're serious about color. They took home "Best of Prosper" in 2025. Once you see their balayage work and gray blending, you'll understand why. Their stylists are certified in Bellami and Malia handtied extensions too, so if you're thinking about going longer, they can help you out.

Prices run mid-range, and they're known for taking their time to get the color exactly right. The team stays constantly educated on new techniques, so you know you're getting current trends done right. Walk-ins are welcome, but with how popular they are, booking ahead saves you the wait.

### 2. Luxe Obsession Salon

Head over to 2281 E University Dr Suite 135 (off the University Drive corridor near Collin County), and you'll find Luxe Obsession Salon. They specialize in color correction, gray coverage, and keeping your color looking fresh between appointments. If you've had a color disaster somewhere else, these folks can help fix it.

They're premium pricing but worth it if you want precision. Brazilian Blowout treatments are on their menu too. That's huge if you want smooth, manageable hair without the frizz. Their team focuses on making sure your hair stays healthy and polished, not just trendy for the moment.

### 3. The Pink House Salon

Downtown at 306 E Broadway St, The Pink House Salon brings an elevated, boutique vibe to Prosper's hair scene. Jacinda and her team specialize in custom color work and are known for listening carefully to what you actually want, not what they think you should want. Their color services lean luxury, and extensions are a big part of their business.

You'll pay premium prices, but clients leave feeling like the best version of themselves. They're perfect if you want to feel pampered while you're getting your hair done.

## Best for Cuts & Styling

<img src="/images/blog/salons-styling.webp" alt="Hair stylist with tools at Prosper TX salon" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin:1.5rem 0;" />

### 4. Prosper Hair

Over at 821 N Coleman St, Prosper Hair is the neighborhood workhorse salon that's been building a solid reputation with real results. Michelle Sansone runs the place with her husband Sal, and they've got a talented crew of stylists including China, Brittany, and Emily. Whether you want a sharp women's cut, men's fade, or a full style transformation, they deliver.

What sets them apart is the consistency and the relationships they build with clients. They do color too, but if you're coming for precision cuts and everyday styling, this is the spot. Pricing is reasonable, and they welcome both walk-ins and appointments. They understand that in Prosper, life's busy, and they work with your schedule.

### 5. TEASE Hair Design

Located at 1061 N Coleman St (same strip as Prosper Hair basically), TEASE Hair Design is super welcoming to walk-ins. That's clutch if you need a cut and can't book far in advance. Kim Irwin is one of their standout stylists. Customers rave about her consistency and friendly demeanor. They handle precision cuts, color, and styling for all hair types.

The vibe is laid-back but professional, and prices are competitive. If you're in the North Coleman corridor around Windsong Ranch or Star Trail neighborhoods and need something quick and quality, TEASE is reliable.

### 6. Hair with Hope

If you've got thick hair or texture that's hard to work with, ask for Hope. She specializes in handling textured and voluminous hair and gets it. Her clients trust her completely because she takes the time to understand your hair's unique needs. She's on the Booksy platform if you want to book online and see her exact availability.

Pricing is mid-range, and she's the kind of stylist who'll be honest about what actually works with your hair. She won't just do whatever you ask for. That's the kind of pro move you want.

## Best for Men's Grooming & Barbershops

### 7. Hall of Fadez

Right on Preston Road at 980 S Preston Rd Suite 317 (inside the City Salon Suites building), Hall of Fadez has the barbershop energy with real precision. They're open Thursday-Friday 8am-6pm and Saturday 1pm-4pm (closed Sunday and Monday). If you're looking for a clean fade, sharp lineup, or traditional barbershop service, they deliver.

The barbers know what they're doing, and the atmosphere is genuine barbershop culture. Prices are fair, and they're serious about the craft. Guys around here know Hall of Fadez is solid.

### 8. Boyz to Beardz

Also at 980 S Preston Rd (same building as Hall of Fadez, Suite 115), Boyz to Beardz specializes in men's cuts and beard styling. If you're growing out a beard and want it shaped properly, or you need a fresh cut with intention, they're your people. They handle haircuts and styling for guys of all ages, from kids getting their first real cut to dads looking sharp.

The pricing is straightforward, and the stylists take pride in doing men's grooming right. It's a no-nonsense environment focused on quality cuts.

## Best Boutique & Luxury Salons

### 9. Luxe Obsession Salon (Premium Pick)

We mentioned them for color, but Luxe Obsession really deserves its own spotlight as a luxury destination salon. At 2281 E University Dr, they've created a full-service premium experience. Beyond color and Brazilian Blowouts, they do precision cuts for men and women, extensions, and styling.

The whole concept is designed to keep your hair looking polished between visits. They offer hair maintenance services, not just one-off appointments. If you're willing to invest in your hair care, this salon treats it like an actual practice, not just a quick errand.

### 10. The Pink House Salon (Premium Pick)

The Pink House at 306 E Broadway St is where you go if you want the full salon experience as an event, not a transaction. They're known for custom extensions and custom color. They treat every head of hair like it's unique (because it is). Jacinda's approach is collaborative, which makes a difference.

This is a destination salon in Prosper, not just a cut-and-go place. Just know: premium pricing applies. If luxury services matter to you, the execution here matches the cost.
