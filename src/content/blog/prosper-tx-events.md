If you're looking for things to do in Prosper this month, you're in for a treat. Spring is in full swing, and our little town is packed with free community events, live music, family-friendly activities, and local farmers markets. Whether you're new to the area or a longtime local, here's your complete guide to Prosper TX events this month.

## Weekly Recurring Events

<img src="/images/blog/events-weekly.webp" alt="Fresh produce at Prosper TX weekly farmers market" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin:1.5rem 0;" />

### 1. Prosper Farmers Market

**When:** Saturdays, 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM
**Where:** 906 W. Broadway St., Prosper, TX
**Cost:** Free to browse and shop
**What to expect:** Fresh local produce, handmade crafts, baked goods, and unique items from local vendors. It's a great way to support Prosper farmers and artisans while grabbing quality ingredients for the week.

### 2. Downtown Prosper Strolls

**When:** Most weekends (check specific dates with town)
**Where:** Downtown Prosper, Broadway Street
**Cost:** Free
**What to expect:** Walk around town, visit local shops and [restaurants](/blog/best-restaurants-in-prosper-tx/), enjoy the spring weather. Many businesses host special promotions and there's usually live music happening at various spots.

### 3. Chrome and Coffee

**When:** Monthly (typically mid-month, check prospertx.gov for exact dates)
**Where:** Downtown Prosper
**Cost:** Free
**What to expect:** Classic car enthusiasts gather to show off their rides, grab coffee from local shops, and chat with fellow car lovers. It's a casual, laid-back vibe that draws folks from all over the area.

## April 2026 Events

<img src="/images/blog/events-april.webp" alt="People celebrating at Prosper TX community event" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin:1.5rem 0;" />

### 4. EGGstravaganza (Easter Egg Hunt)

**When:** Early April (check town events calendar for exact date)
**Where:** 4300 E First St., Prosper, TX
**Cost:** Free
**What to expect:** This is one of the biggest kid-friendly events of the spring. Expect Easter egg hunts for different age groups, bounce houses, face painters, and tons of activities that'll keep the little ones entertained for hours. Bring your camera because this one gets packed.

### 5. Spring Fling Community Event

**When:** Saturday, April 4, 2026, 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM
**Where:** 5600 W University Dr., Prosper
**Cost:** Free
**What to expect:** A community gathering featuring family activities, local vendors, and a chance to meet your neighbors. Spring Fling is perfect if you're looking for a relaxed afternoon outside. There's usually something for every age, from games to live entertainment.

### 6. Egg Surprise Event

**When:** Saturday, April 4, 2026, 10:30 AM - 12:30 PM
**Where:** 1091 S Preston Rd., Prosper, TX 75078
**Cost:** Free
**What to expect:** Another Easter-themed event with egg hunts and surprise activities. Great for families who want to get out early in the morning and beat the crowds at other events.

### 7. Taylor Davidson Live Music

**When:** Friday, April 3, 2026, 7:00 PM onwards
**Where:** 1440 West Frontier Parkway, Prosper, TX
**Cost:** Free entry (food and drinks available for purchase)
**What to expect:** Live acoustic music from local artist Taylor Davidson. This is a casual, outdoor evening event perfect for grabbing dinner from [local restaurants](/food-and-fun/) and enjoying some tunes as the sun sets. Dog-friendly, so bring your pup.

### 8. Dave Christen Duo w/ Don Maracle

**When:** Saturday, April 4, 2026, 7:00 PM
**Where:** The Brass Tap, Prosper
**Cost:** No cover charge
**What to expect:** The Brass Tap hosts local musicians regularly, and this show promises good drinks, great company, and solid live music. It's the spot to head to if you want a more laid-back evening with folks from around town.

### 9. Wine Tasting and Live Acoustic Music

**When:** Friday, April 3, 2026, Evening
**Where:** Downtown Prosper venue (check calendar for specific location)
**Cost:** Free entry
**What to expect:** A family and dog-friendly event combining wine tasting with live acoustic music. Perfect for those looking for a sophisticated but casual evening out.

### 10. Prosper ISD FFA Plant Sale & Farmers Market

**When:** Saturday, March 28 (note: falls in late March but worth mentioning if you're planning ahead)
**Where:** 301 Eagle Drive, Prosper, TX
**Cost:** Free entry (plants for sale)
**What to expect:** Support our local schools while picking up plants and fresh items from local vendors. The FFA does incredible work in our community, and this event is a great way to show support while doing your spring planting.

## Annual Prosper Events Worth Marking on Your Calendar

<img src="/images/blog/events-annual.webp" alt="Crowd at annual outdoor festival in Prosper TX" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin:1.5rem 0;" />

While these aren't happening in April, mark your calendar now because they're some of the biggest celebrations in Prosper:

- **Prosper on Tap: Blooms and Bubbly** (May 9): A spring celebration featuring craft beverages, food, and local vendors
- **Memorial Day Ceremony** (May 25): A solemn tribute to those who served
- **Downtown Block Party** (May 30): Live music, food trucks, and community fun right downtown
- **Independence Day Celebration** (July 2): Complete with parade, fireworks, live music, and carnival games
- **Celebrate Prosper** (October 17): Our annual hometown party in the park with live music, Kid Zones, and community exhibitors
- **Prosper Christmas Festival** (December 5): Holiday shopping, ice skating, holiday inflatables, and holiday magic

For a complete list of what's happening throughout the year, check the [2026 Prosper Calendar of Events](https://www.prospertx.gov/222/2026-Prosper-Calendar-of-Events) on the town website. You can also browse our [Prosper guide](/prosper-guide/) for more on what makes this town special.
