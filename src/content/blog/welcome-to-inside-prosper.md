## A New Way to Discover Local

If you live in Prosper, Texas, you already know this is a special community. The kind of place where neighbors wave, businesses know your name, and supporting local is more than a bumper sticker — it's how we live.

**Inside Prosper** was built to make discovering those local businesses easier. We are not a review site, and we are not a paid directory. Every business featured here was invited because they are doing great work and the community trusts them.

## What You Will Find Here

Our directory covers five key categories that serve the Prosper community:

- **Food & Fun** — Restaurants, coffee shops, and local eateries
- **Health & Fitness** — Gyms, studios, and wellness professionals
- **Professionals** — Lawyers, CPAs, and business services
- **Shop** — Boutiques, florists, and retail stores
- **Trades** — Plumbers, electricians, and skilled contractors

Each business gets a dedicated profile with their story, contact info, hours, and what makes them worth supporting.

## Why Invitation-Only?

We made a deliberate choice to keep Inside Prosper invitation-only. That means you are not seeing who paid the most to show up. You are seeing businesses that earned their place by doing honest, quality work for the community.

We think that matters. And based on the response so far, our neighbors agree.
